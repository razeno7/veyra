About Veyra, the trader behind the signals

I’m Veyra. I’ve been trading futures for more than 7 years, and NQ, the E-mini Nasdaq-100 future, is the market I trade live for the Alpha Membership.

There’s no team of analysts behind Veyra Trading: one trader, one market, signals called live while I trade.

What I do every weekday

Monday to Friday, from the 9:30 a.m. New York open, I trade NQ live on stream for the first 2–3 hours of the US session. I call every signal myself, in real time, inside the private Discord: direction, entry, stop and target. That’s what the membership gives you: my NQ futures signals, the live sessions where I call them, and access to the Discord.

How I keep score

I personally track the R of every signal I call. R measures a result in units of the risk taken: the distance from entry to stop is 1R, so a stopped-out signal is −1R and a signal that reaches a target twice that distance is +2R.

That same record decides the positive-R refund. If the total R of my signals over your billing month is zero or negative, that month’s $200 is refunded in full. It isn’t personalised investment advice, and nothing is tailored to your financial situation. Signals and live sessions are about my own trading. Your results can differ from the signals because of execution, timing, slippage, position size, fees and your own decisions.

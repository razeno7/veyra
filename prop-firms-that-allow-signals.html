Prop firms that allow signals: what five futures firms’ rules say

By Veyra · Rules read on 11 November 2026 · About Veyra

On 11 November 2026 I read the published rules of five futures prop firms to see which ones allow you to follow a signal provider. None of the five says in writing that it’s allowed. One prohibits it by name.

I sell NQ signals, so this isn’t the answer that suits me. It’s what the firms’ own pages say. Each quote below links to the page it comes from, so you can check it yourself.

The Veyra Prop Firm Signal Rules Check, in numbers

  • 0 of 5 firms say in writing that following a third-party signal provider is allowed.
  • 1 of 5 (BluSky) prohibits copying trades from a signal provider by name.
  • 3 of 5 (Alpha Futures, BluSky, Tradeify) prohibit copying another person’s trades in writing.
  • 2 of 5 (Take Profit Trader, Topstep) have no such rule in the pages I read. One says decisions and execution must remain your own, the other prohibits coordinated trading.

Firms read: Alpha Futures, BluSky, Take Profit Trader, Topstep and Tradeify. A sixth, Apex Trader Funding, isn’t counted: I couldn’t retrieve its rules page that day.

Alpha Futures

“the only copy trading allowed is that from your own external account to your Alpha Futures Account. Any Analyst found copy trading other analyst’s trades will be subject to review and if found to be in breach of the Virtual Trading Rules, will result in instant termination of the Services and the closure of your Alpha Futures Account”

Source: Alpha Futures terms and conditions, clause 8.1.5.

“Semi-Automated Trading, such as a custom indicator that gives buy or sell signals, is permissible under the condition that the user manually places, monitors, and manages the trade”

Source: Alpha Futures, Prohibited Trading Practices.

Alpha Futures calls its traders analysts. The signals it allows here come from an indicator. The page doesn’t say anything separate about calls made by a person.

BluSky

BluSky’s conduct policy lists this as forbidden:

“Copying trades from another trader, account, or signal provider, as this undermines independent trading skill development and risk management.”

Source: BluSky, Ethical Trading Conduct Policy.

It’s the only one of the five whose rules name signal providers.

Take Profit Trader

“Many prop firms, including Take Profit Trader, require that all trading decisions and execution remain independent.”

“Traders are welcome to draw inspiration from other traders’ ideas and approaches, but the actual decision-making and execution must remain their own.”

Source: Take Profit Trader blog, What Is a Trade Copier?

This is the firm’s blog, not its rulebook. It’s the only place I found the firm writing about signal providers.

Topstep

Topstep’s prohibited conduct page doesn’t mention signals. It prohibits coordinated trading, described as:

“performing trades in concert with others (including unconnected accounts or third parties) to pool risk, hedge aggregate positions, or trade the same or opposite strategy simultaneously”

Source: Topstep, Prohibited Conduct.

Tradeify

“Team trading, collaborative trading, group trading, the management of a Trader’s account by or on behalf of any third party, and the copying or mirroring of trades between the Trader and any other person are strictly prohibited.”

Source: Tradeify funded trader agreement, section 6.7(b).

The same section says all trading decisions must reflect the trader’s individual strategy and discretion.

Apex Trader Funding

I couldn’t retrieve Apex’s rules page on 11 November 2026, so it isn’t in the count. Read its prohibited activities page yourself.

What this means if you follow signals

None of these pages says “you may follow a signal provider if you place each order by hand”. Most of them talk about copying trades and about making your own decisions. Whether taking a live call by hand counts as copying is the firm’s decision, not mine and not yours.

So ask your firm’s support in writing before you follow anyone’s signals on a challenge or funded account, and keep the answer. If the answer is no, don’t use signals on that account.
